# The mightyweenie logo (the project's own art)

`mightyweenie_logo.png` (110 x 26 pixels) is the project's own logo, drawn by
its author for mightyweenie. It is the port's art: not ROM data and not from
the original game. The main menu shows it under the original's logo at its
own size, pixel for pixel (imported lossless, unfiltered).

`assets/logo/mightyweenie_logo_3x.png` (330 x 78) is the same image scaled
3x, nearest neighbour, for the top of the README.
